# Code of Conduct

Fluxygram is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want every issue, discussion and pull request to be a place where anyone can take part without being harassed or put down.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in their second language.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it in a way you'd like to receive it.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and stay on topic.

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual language, imagery or attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing conversations.

## Where it applies

In every Fluxygram space: the repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, tell the maintainers privately: [open a private report](https://github.com/Fluxygram/fluxygram/security/advisories/new) with "Conduct" in the title (only maintainers can see it), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are handled confidentially.

## What happens next

Maintainers review every report and decide on a response that fits what happened: a private conversation, a public warning, removing comments, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces. Maintainers who don't follow this code may be removed from the project.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
