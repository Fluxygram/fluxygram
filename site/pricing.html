Questions

Can I cancel?

Yes, whenever you want, from your account. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my diagrams if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. You can open, edit and share all of them; you just can't save new ones beyond the free 3 until you delete some.

Who charges me?

Payments are handled by Polar, our merchant of record. They charge the card, send the invoice and handle taxes for your country. We never see your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Pro isn't for you, write to hello@fluxygram.app within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund it.

What is the founder price?

The price for the first subscribers. It stays the same for you while your subscription is active, even when Pro gets new features and its price goes up.
